CLINIC X - How to open with one click (Windows)
==============================================

FIRST TIME ONLY
1. Install Node.js from https://nodejs.org
   (Choose LTS version, then Next Next Install)

2. Unzip ClinicX-Desktop-App.zip

3. Open the folder ClinicX-App

4. Double-click:  Create-Desktop-Icon.bat
   → This puts "Clinic X" icon on your Desktop

EVERY DAY
- Double-click the Desktop icon:  Clinic X
- Login and use the system
